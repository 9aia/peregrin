Event-driven orchestration for agent workflows — nodes, campaigns, and remote transport across your machines.

Architecture

Nodes

A daemon per machine hosts the session plane (via sepia serve) and campaign drivers — one install, two capability planes.

Campaigns

Declarative workflows over agents — graph-walking engine, YAML loader, templating, durable runs.

Hub

Aggregation across nodes — merged entity/session views, sync, lock arbitration, and fleet dispatch.
