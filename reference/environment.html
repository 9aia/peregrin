Environment ​

Every variable peregrin reads. PEREGRIN_* configures peregrin itself; SEPIA_* vars are consumed by sepia binaries (the session-plane child and its drivers) — those are documented in sepia's CLI reference.

The generic substrate reads HYPHA_* names — peregrin serve and peregrin-hub translate the PEREGRIN_* contract onto them at the boundary (PEREGRIN_NODES → HYPHA_NODES, PEREGRIN_CREDENTIAL_STORE → HYPHA_CREDENTIAL_STORE, PEREGRIN_EPHEMERAL_DEAD_AFTER → HYPHA_EPHEMERAL_DEAD_AFTER, PEREGRIN_HOME → HYPHA_HOME under ~/.local/share/peregrin).
